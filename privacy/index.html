Rhodes & Rudyard: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026 Developer: Isaac Hirsch Contact for privacy questions: isaachirsch@gmail.com

The short version

Rhodes & Rudyard is a children's game. We do not collect any personal information from you or your child. Everything the app remembers stays on your device. There are no accounts, no adverts, no tracking, no in-app purchases and no links out of the app.

What the app keeps on your device

  • An explorer nickname, if you choose to type one, and the age group you select.
  • Safari Passport stamps and game progress.
  • Your sound settings.
  • A scrambled check for the Grown-Ups PIN, held in the device's secure storage. The PIN itself is not stored.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it.

What the app does not use

The app does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos. It contains no advertising or analytics software and does not use advertising identifiers.

Device features

  • Spoken instructions are recordings included in the app. A few animal names in other languages are read by your device's own voice for that language, when the device has one. Nothing you say is ever heard or recorded: the app has no microphone access.
  • If a grown-up forgets the PIN, the app asks the device to confirm its owner with the device passcode, fingerprint or face. The device answers only yes or no. The app never receives fingerprint or face data.

Backups

On iPhone and iPad, the app's saved progress is included in your own device backup (iCloud or computer) if you have backups turned on, in the same way as other apps. On Android, the app is excluded from Google account backup.

Keeping and deleting information

Information stays on the device until you delete it. To delete it, open Grown-Ups, then Privacy, then "Delete all data on this device", or uninstall the app. Because we hold no information, there is nothing for us to delete on request, but you can contact us with any question.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children and is intended to be suitable for families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app does not ask a child for any. The Grown-Ups area is protected by a PIN.

Recordings and photographs

Animal recordings and wildlife photographs are credited inside the app under Grown-Ups, About, Wildlife Sound Credits and Wildlife Photo Credits.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date. If a future version of the app ever collected information, we would update this policy first and ask a parent or guardian where the law requires it.
