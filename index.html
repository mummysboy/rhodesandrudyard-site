Rhodes & Rudyard

A children's safari game. Explore with Rhodes and Rudyard, meet animals from every continent, hear their real calls, find where they live, learn their names in other languages, and collect them all in your Safari Passport.

Need help? Email isaachirsch@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

For grown-ups

  • No accounts, ads, tracking or purchases. Everything the app remembers stays on your device. See the privacy policy.
  • Grown-Ups area. Tap Grown-Ups on the home screen. The first time, answer a multiplication question and choose a 4-digit PIN. After that, the PIN opens it.
  • Forgotten PIN. In Grown-Ups, tap "I forgot the PIN" and confirm with your device passcode, Face ID or Touch ID. Your child's progress is kept.
  • Change the age group or sound. Both are in Grown-Ups.
  • Delete everything. Grown-Ups, then Privacy, then "Delete all data on this device", or delete the app.
  • Movement games. Move Like Me asks children to copy animal moves. Please make sure there is clear space around them. Every move can be skipped, and nothing is filmed or checked.
